Explanation:
This challenge demonstrates LSB (Least Significant Bit) steganography.
Images consist of pixels, where each pixel typically has Red, Green, and Blue (RGB) color channels represented by 8-bit values (0-255). 
Because changing the lowest bit (the least significant bit) of these color values alters the color by an imperceptible amount to the human eye, it is a common place to hide secret data.

To retrieve the flag:
1. We iterated through the pixels in the image.
2. We extracted the least significant bit (using a bitwise AND operation with 1, i.e., `value & 1`) from the Red channel of each pixel.
3. We collected these bits sequentially and grouped them into chunks of 8 to form bytes.
4. Finally, converting these bytes into ASCII characters revealed the hidden message.
